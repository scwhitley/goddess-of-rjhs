Our Privacy Promise

The foundation of the RJHS platform begins and ends with trust and we pride ourselves in protecting the information of all clients. Our company embodies two main values as we strive to develop and maintain relationships with each client.

Trust

We want you to trust that anything discussed between you and any of our staff members will not be discussed externally. We respect, and value your privacy. We understand your information belongs to you!

Information Security

We will not share/release your personal information with any outside/third party vendor unless consent is received from the client or legally mandated by the court of law. When we are legally required to share your personal information, we will tell you in advance, unless we are legally forbidden by a judge or court mandated documents.
