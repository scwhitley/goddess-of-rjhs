Meet Janee

Welcome to RJHS Holistic Healing & Wellness.

I’m Janee, Goddess Of RJHS, an End of Life Doula, Usui/Holy Fire III Reiki Master Teacher, Shamanic Practitioner, and Intuitive Spiritual Coach dedicated to helping people reconnect with themselves through healing, clarity, and spiritual alignment.

Shamanic PractitionerIntuitive CoachCertified End-of-Life (EOL) DoulaAngelic Reiki®Usui/Holy Fire III Reiki Master-Teacher (RMT)

Through grief, life transitions, and spiritual awakening, I learned what it truly means to heal while still showing up for others. That journey inspired me to create a space where people can feel seen, supported, and safe while navigating their own healing process.

Whether you are seeking energetic support, spiritual guidance, emotional balance, grief support, or simply a moment to breathe and reconnect, my intention is to meet you exactly where you are with compassion, authenticity, and care.

You do not have to navigate healing, grief, or spiritual growth alone.

This space was created for healing, growth, grounding, and transformation. I invite you to look around, explore the offerings, and trust whatever guided you here in the first place.

Your healing is sacred.— Janee, Goddess Of RJHS

    Our Privacy Promise

    The foundation of the RJHS platform begins and ends with trust and we pride ourselves in protecting the information of all clients. Our company embodies two main values as we strive to develop and maintain relationships with each client.

    Trust

    We want you to trust that anything discussed between you and any of our staff members will not be discussed externally. We respect, and value your privacy. We understand your information belongs to you!

    Information Security

    We will not share/release your personal information with any outside/third party vendor unless consent is received from the client or legally mandated by the court of law. When we are legally required to share your personal information, we will tell you in advance, unless we are legally forbidden by a judge or court mandated documents.
